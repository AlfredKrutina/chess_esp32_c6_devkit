Detailed Description

Square to SK6812 index for the quadrant chain.

Four identical 4x4 boards. Chain starts at e1. Each next board is rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise from the previous one. 0-15 e1-h4 0 deg e1..e4, f4..f1, g1..g4, h4..h1 16-31 e5-h8 90 CCW 32-47 a5-d8 180 48-63 a1-d4 270 CCW (quadrant not fitted on the bench)
