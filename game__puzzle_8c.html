Detailed Description

Puzzle session: physical setup, gated start, line check, LED feedback.

The app stages a FEN plus a solver-first UCI line (solver, opponent, solver…). Play does not begin until the matrix matches that FEN. Opponent plies are shown on LEDs and must be executed on the board so logic and wood stay together.
