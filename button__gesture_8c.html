Detailed Description

Debounced one-button gestures for SW1 on the V2 hall PCB.

Short click is a press shorter than 400 ms. A second press inside 400 ms that is held becomes power-off (it wins over a triple-click and over new-game). Three short clicks start pairing after a short quiet. Four short clicks start the animation show. Two clicks step the lamp color. A hold that begins with no click in the previous 400 ms starts the new-game snake. The 1 s legacy long-press threshold is not a commit point.
