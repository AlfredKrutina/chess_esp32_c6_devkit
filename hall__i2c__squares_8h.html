Detailed Description

I2C Hall occupancy for the 64 squares, plus an uncalibrated piece-ID POC.

Occupancy (0/1) is the product scan path used by squares_task. Piece type and color are a separate proof-of-concept ported from HAL_czechmate_POC. Those thresholds are not a calibrated product classifier; do not treat a Hall type/color as ground truth. Reed builds (Hall Kconfig off) keep the same entry points and return empty/usable defaults.
