Education

Why CzechMate is a learning system — not just a “smart board”

Chess clicks best when the pieces are under your hands and you can see right away whether a move makes sense. CzechMate brings together real pieces, lights on the squares, and an app with rules, position, and analysis. Set your opponent from calm lessons up to a top engine; when you need an explanation, bring in the AI coach.

01

Every age group

Kids find their bearings with colors on the board; adults get depth of analysis and a calm game pace. Parents, after-school clubs, cafés, and home clubs — one board, many ways to learn.

02

Every skill level

Beginners see where they may move and what check or mate means. Advanced players work on tactics, plans, and evaluations — including a top chess engine and coach conversations.

03

Learning with every sense

You touch a piece, see a lit square, and optionally hear a human explanation or read an AI reply. The more senses you use, the better rules and patterns stick.

The set

Printed pieces the board can identify

The set is faceted PLA: wide feet, thick tips, and no overhang that needs support. Magnets in each foot are arranged so Hall sensors under the square do not only see “something is here” — they resolve which piece type it is (pawn through king), so the position on the board matches the game.

The board as tutor

Lights on the squares show what you need right now

Under every square are Hall sensors. The board knows a piece is there, and the magnet pattern in the foot tells it what type it is — not a guess from the app, a reading from the hardware. Hints land on a real chessboard, not “some picture.” The board runs the game in real time and the LEDs match the pieces. You can refresh firmware without cables to the chip from the app, the board’s web UI, or Wi‑Fi.

From the first game to master habits

Physical pieces and lights on the squares stay in sync: on every move you know whether the system sees what you see. Less guessing, more confidence. Connect a phone or computer and you get a game overview, move history, and hints from the board web UI and the CzechMate app on the same position.

An opponent from another league

Stockfish — play a system stronger at chess than any human alive

CzechMate runs Stockfish: an engine that, in raw strength, beats any living player. For learning, pick a weaker engine level and a calmer game with a readable pace. Start a game from the app or the board’s web UI over Wi‑Fi. On every move you get engine suggestions, position analysis, and a comparison with your choice.

AI coach in the app

AI coach: strategy, mistakes, and a plan right at the board

In the app you get a conversation on the current position: a multi-move plan, typical mistakes, and deeper strategy. Set the explanation level so the text fits a beginner or a club player.

Your AI, under your control

In settings you choose an assistant: cloud based on your account, or a local mode that fits school, club, or home rules. Offline or without a sign-in you still get basic offline hints from built-in chess tips.

CzechMate app

One client for board connection, learning, and analysis

One app for Android and macOS: game overview, board connection over Bluetooth or network, learning, play, Stockfish analysis (with strength control), and the AI coach. No hopping between programs. In settings you can also update board firmware without a cable to a computer.

Download

Download CzechMate and start learning on a living board

Get the latest app for Android (APK), Mac with Apple Silicon (DMG), or Windows (EXE installer). Links go to GitHub Releases and offer a direct download of the current build for your platform. On Android you may need to allow installs from unknown sources if you are not using the Play Store. On Mac, open the DMG and drag the app into Applications. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn about an unsigned package on first run — continue only for files from this project’s official release. The Windows build connects to the board over Wi‑Fi (there is no Bluetooth scan in the desktop app).
