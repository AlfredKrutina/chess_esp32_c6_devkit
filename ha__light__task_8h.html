Detailed Description

HA Light Task - Home Assistant RGB Light Integration.

autotoc_md27

WHAT DID THIS COMPONENT DO?

This task integrates the box as an RGB light into the Home Assistant via MQTT. After connecting to the WiFi STA, the board automatically switches to HA mode after 5 minutes idleness. In HA mode, all LED boards behave as one RGB light.

Modes:

  • GAME MODE: LED displays chest (default)
  • HA MODE: All 64 LEDs as RGB light controlled via HA (after 5 min of inactivity)

Automatic switching:

  • GAME -> HA: After 5 minutes of no activity (figure movement or game command)
  • HA -> GAME: Immediately upon detection of piece movement (PICKUP/DROP)
